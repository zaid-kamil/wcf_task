Alignment Examples

Left Alignment

This is an example of left-aligned text. This text is aligned to the left margin. This is commonly used for paragraphs and most textual content on websites as it is the easiest to read for languages that read from left to right.

Center Alignment

This is an example of center-aligned text. This text is aligned to the center. This type of alignment is often used for titles, headings, and short pieces of text that need emphasis.

Right Alignment

This is an example of right-aligned text. This text is aligned to the right margin. Right alignment is sometimes used for languages that read from right to left or for specific design elements.

Justify Alignment

This is an example of justified text. In justified text, both the left and right edges of the text are aligned with the margins. This creates a clean, rectangular block of text. Justified text is often used in newspapers, magazines, and books to create a formal, orderly appearance.
