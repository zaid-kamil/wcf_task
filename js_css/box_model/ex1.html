Paragraph Padding Examples

First paragraph with left padding: 3cm
This paragraph has a left padding of 3 centimeters. Notice how the text is pushed away from the left edge of the paragraph's box.

Second paragraph with right padding: 50%
This paragraph has a right padding of 50%. This means half of the paragraph's width is used as padding on the right side.

Third paragraph with bottom padding: 25%
This paragraph has a bottom padding of 25%. Notice the extra space at the bottom of this paragraph. The size is 25% of the width of the containing element.

Fourth paragraph with top padding: 25%
This paragraph has a top padding of 25%. Notice the extra space at the top of this paragraph. The size is 25% of the width of the containing element.
